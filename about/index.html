CREDIBILITY & PURPOSE

Why FIX-PLUG? Why Kano?

In every neighbourhood in Kano, finding an honest electrician or plumber is a headache. We are changing that by rebuilding trust from the ground up.

The Problem We Lived Through in Kano

Kano is the commercial heartbeat of Northern Nigeria. Tens of thousands of modern homes, apartments, and businesses rely on boreholes, overhead tanks, generators, inverters, and electrical grids every single day.

Yet when something breaks, what happens? You ask your neighbor. You call a roadside number. Sometimes someone shows up four hours late. Sometimes they buy substandard parts, quote triple the real price, or take an advance payment and switch off their phone.

At the same time, honest, talented Kano tradespeople struggle to find steady work because a few dishonest operators have ruined customer trust.

FIX-PLUG was founded to fix this broken link. We stand between both sides as an impartial guarantor of quality, safety, and fairness.

Our Core Values

Four simple principles guide every decision we make.

Honesty Over Profit

We never inflate material prices or make promises we cannot keep. If a job is beyond our scope, we say so upfront.

Respect for Artisans

Artisans build our cities. We ensure they are treated with dignity, paid promptly, and protected from arbitrary cuts.

In-Person Rigour

Trust cannot be established over an internet form alone. We meet every artisan in person and test their hands-on work.

Accountability

If something goes wrong on a job, we don't pass the blame. We step in, evaluate the facts, and see that it is made right.

Who Is Behind FIX-PLUG?

FIX-PLUG is built by a focused team of founders with hands-on backgrounds in operations, supply vetting, technology, and local community engagement in Kano.

  • Operations & Strategy: Experienced founders committed to solving the domestic service gap in Northern Nigeria.
  • Supply & Vetting Lead: On the ground in Kano daily, conducting physical trade assessments and guarantor background checks.
  • Comms & Community: Managing transparent customer communication across WhatsApp, local estate groups, and neighbourhood partnerships.

Terms of Service

Last updated: 26 November 2026. These Terms apply each time you use FIX-PLUG to request a tradesperson or to accept bookings as a tradesperson.

These terms are written in plain English. If anything is unclear, email us at fixplug.ng@gmail.com and we will explain.

1. About FIX-PLUG

FIX-PLUG is a booking platform that connects households and businesses in Kano, Nigeria with vetted, independent tradespeople. We are not an employment agency and we do not employ the tradespeople ourselves. Our role is to verify their identity and skill, coordinate the booking, and act as a trusted middleman between you and the tradesperson.

FIX-PLUG is completing its corporate registration under Nigerian law. Full CAC registration details will be published here and in the site footer once issued.

2. Services We Cover in Kano

FIX-PLUG currently coordinates bookings for the following trades in Kano State:

  • Plumbing — pipe leaks, borehole pump connections, overhead tank installations, toilet and kitchen repairs, drainage blockages.
  • Electrical — inverter and solar wiring, socket replacements, generator changeover switches, fault tracing, distribution board setups.
  • Repairs & Handyman — door locks and hinges, wardrobe repairs, roof and ceiling patches, masonry touch-ups, mounting appliances and TVs.
  • House & Deep Cleaning — residential deep cleaning, post-construction scrubbing, kitchen and tile degreasing, move-in / move-out compound care.

We cover all local government areas within Kano State, including Nassarawa, Fagge, Tarauni, Dala, Gwale, Kano Municipal, Kumbotso, Ungogo, and surrounding areas.

3. How a Booking Works

  1. Request. You submit a job request by WhatsApp message or through our online form. Describe the problem and your location in Kano.
  2. Assignment. Our coordinator reviews your request and assigns a verified tradesperson who is available, local, and skilled in that trade. We contact you to confirm the assignment, usually within a few hours.
  3. Inspection & Quote. The tradesperson visits your property, inspects the problem, and gives you a price before any work begins. You are free to decline the quote — there is no obligation.
  4. Job. You agree to the quote and the tradesperson carries out the work. For larger jobs, the tradesperson may phase the work and quote each phase.
  5. Payment. You pay the tradesperson directly on completion — either cash, bank transfer, or any method you agree with them. Any replacement parts or materials are charged at actual receipt cost, which the tradesperson will show you.

4. Quotes and Pricing

All quotes are given on-site, in person, before work starts. Preliminary price ranges discussed over phone or WhatsApp are rough estimates only — the final price is what the tradesperson quotes after inspecting the job.

No work should begin until you have agreed the price. If you feel pressured to accept a price before seeing the work, contact us immediately.

5. Payments

You pay the tradesperson directly — not FIX-PLUG. FIX-PLUG does not handle or hold your money. If you pay any person claiming to represent FIX-PLUG before the job is done, please report it to us immediately as this may be fraud.

The tradesperson's labour fee and the actual receipt cost of materials are the only costs you should pay. FIX-PLUG does not add a hidden margin on top of materials.

6. Cancellations and Rescheduling

You can cancel or reschedule a booking at no charge before the tradesperson departs for your property. Once the tradesperson is in transit, a reasonable call-out fee may apply to cover their travel cost — your coordinator will tell you the amount before you decide.

If a tradesperson cancels or fails to show up without reasonable notice, tell our coordinator and we will arrange a replacement as quickly as possible at no extra charge to you.

7. Guarantees and Complaints

Every tradesperson in the FIX-PLUG network has passed our in-person vetting process (see Section 9 and our Verification Standard page). However, all tradespeople are independent contractors and are personally responsible for the quality and safety of their work.

If the work is not right, here is what we do:

  • Contact our coordinator immediately — WhatsApp 0813 927 7393 or email fixplug.ng@gmail.com.
  • We will listen to both you and the tradesperson and assess the facts fairly.
  • Where the fault is on the tradesperson's side, we will arrange a return visit or a replacement tradesperson to put it right.
  • Persistent or serious misconduct by a tradesperson results in removal from the FIX-PLUG network.

We aim to respond to all complaints within 24 hours on working days.

8. What We Cannot Guarantee

While we take artisan vetting seriously, we cannot guarantee:

  • That a tradesperson will be available immediately in every trade or neighbourhood — especially during our growth phase.
  • That the tradesperson's work will be free of any defect — workmanship liability rests with the individual tradesperson.
  • Outcomes in cases where both parties tell conflicting stories and there is no independent evidence.

9. Artisan Verification

Before any tradesperson is dispatched to a customer, they must pass all of the following:

  • Identity check — government-issued ID (National ID, voter's card, or driver's licence), matched in person against the applicant's face.
  • Practical skills test — a hands-on demonstration of their trade, assessed in person by our vetting team.
  • Guarantor check — a confirmed home guarantor resident in Kano who vouches for the applicant's identity and character.

Tradespeople who pass all three checks are issued an internal FIX-PLUG approval code and may be dispatched to customers. Approval can be revoked at any time for misconduct.

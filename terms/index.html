Terms of Service

Effective Date: November 2026 | Phase 1 Early Access in Kano State, Nigeria

1. Service Status in Kano (Phase 1 Early Access)

FIX-PLUG coordinates connections between households/businesses and vetted independent tradespeople in Kano State. During our Phase 1 early-access rollout, we are actively onboarding and vetting electricians, plumbers, and handymen. Submitting a request places your job into our priority dispatch queue for coordinator review and does not constitute an unconditional guarantee of immediate artisan availability in every trade or neighbourhood.

2. Pricing, Estimates & Material Receipts

Preliminary price ranges discussed over phone or WhatsApp are non-binding estimates. Final labour charges and any required replacement parts are agreed directly on-site between the customer and the tradesperson after physical inspection and before work begins. Customers pay actual store-receipt costs for replacement materials.

3. Dispute Support & Limitations

Independent tradespeople remain responsible for the quality and safety of their workmanship. If an issue arises on a dispatched job, our coordinator team will review the facts with both parties and assist in mediating a fair resolution, including re-inspection or reassignment where feasible.

4. Professional Standards & Respect

Both customers and artisans are expected to maintain respectful, safe, and honest conduct on every job. Safety violations, unapproved price inflation after agreement, or failure to honour confirmed appointments may result in removal from the FIX-PLUG network.

5. Privacy & Data Protection

Your personal data is handled in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023. Please read our Privacy Notice for full details.

6. Corporate Registration Notice

FIX-PLUG is completing its corporate registration filings in Nigeria. Official Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) entity details will be published here and in the platform footer upon issuance.

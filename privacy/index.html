LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

Privacy Notice & Terms of Service

Last updated: 20 November 2026 | Effective for FIX-PLUG Phase 1

1. Data Protection Notice (NDPA 2023)

This notice explains in plain language how FIX-PLUG collects, uses, and safeguards personal data in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

A. What We Collect on This Website

We believe in minimal data collection. We only ask for what is strictly necessary to arrange tradesperson services or review artisan applications:

  • For Customers: Name, phone / WhatsApp number, neighbourhood area in Kano, service needed (plumbing, electrical, repairs), and a brief description of the issue.
  • For Artisans: Name, phone number, trade skill, area of Kano, years of trade experience, and general certification status.
Strict Rule: FIX-PLUG does not collect National Identity Numbers (NIN), bank account details, or identity card uploads through this public website. All identity checks occur in person during our physical vetting process.

B. How We Use Your Data

Your details are used solely to:

  • Contact you regarding your service request or artisan application.
  • Connect customers with verified tradespeople in Kano.
  • Maintain internal dispatch records. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers or third parties.

C. Storage and Security

Submissions are stored in secured database storage accessible exclusively by authorized FIX-PLUG team members. We employ rate limiting and anti-spam measures (including honeypot traps) to protect our forms.

D. Data Retention & Your Rights

Under the NDPA 2023, you have the right to request access to your data, correct inaccuracies, or request the complete deletion of your records from our systems. Customer service requests that do not proceed into active jobs are automatically purged after 90 days. To request deletion, email privacy@fixplug.ng.

2. Terms of Service (Phase 1)

A. Service Status: Launch Phase in Kano

FIX-PLUG is currently in its initial launch phase in Kano, Nigeria. During this phase, submissions join a curated dispatch priority list. Submitting a request does not constitute an instant contract or automatic guarantee of immediate artisan dispatch.

B. Pricing and Quotations

Any preliminary price indications are estimates. Final labor charges and material requirements are agreed directly between the customer and the artisan on-site after physical inspection, prior to work commencement.

C. Content Accuracy

FIX-PLUG upholds the highest truth-in-advertising standards. We do not publish simulated job counts, unverified ratings, or stock imagery presented as our verified workers.

D. Corporate Registration Notice

FIX-PLUG is completing its corporate registration filings in Nigeria. Legal name details and Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) registration numbers will appear in the site footer upon formal confirmation.

E. Contact for Legal Inquiries

For legal or regulatory questions regarding data protection or platform terms, please write to: hello@fixplug.ng.

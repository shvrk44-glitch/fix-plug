Privacy Notice & Terms of Service

Last updated: 20 November 2026 | Effective for FIX-PLUG Phase 1

1. Data Protection & Privacy Notice (NDPA 2023 Compliant)

This Privacy Notice is issued in full compliance with Section 27 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and the directives of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). It explains how FIX-PLUG ("we", "our", or "the Platform") collects, processes, protects, and disposes of personal data across our tradesperson dispatch ecosystem in Kano State, Nigeria.

A. Identity of the Data Controller

Data Controller: FIX-PLUG Marketplace Operations
Location: Kano, Kano State, Nigeria
Email Contact: fix-plug.ng@gmail.com
WhatsApp Coordinator: +234 813 927 7393

B. Categories of Personal Data Collected

Under the principle of data minimization, FIX-PLUG collects only information strictly required to match customers with verified artisans:

  • Customer Data: Full name, active WhatsApp/phone number, neighbourhood/area in Kano (e.g. Nassarawa, Tarauni, Fagge), trade service requested, and problem description.
  • Artisan Data: Full name, phone/WhatsApp number, primary trade discipline, operational LGA in Kano, trade experience duration, and certification status.
Mandatory Protection Protocol: FIX-PLUG does not collect National Identity Numbers (NIN), biometric fingerprints, or bank account credentials through this public web portal. All identity verification takes place in person during our physical vetting assessment in Kano.

C. Lawful Bases for Data Processing (Section 25 NDPA)

Every processing operation conducted by FIX-PLUG is anchored on valid statutory grounds under the NDPA:

  • Consent: Freely given, specific, and unambiguous consent obtained via an explicit affirmative opt-in checkbox before submission.
  • Performance of a Contract: Processing necessary to take preliminary steps at your request to dispatch a qualified local artisan to your location.
  • Legitimate Interests: Ensuring job-site security, verifying trade competencies, preventing spam/fraud, and maintaining marketplace integrity.
  • Legal Obligation: Compliance with Nigerian tax and statutory regulatory requests where mandated by law.

D. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

Customer job details (name, phone number, and neighborhood location) are shared exclusively with the specific verified tradesperson assigned to your job for the sole purpose of service fulfillment. We strictly never sell, rent, commercialize, or share personal data with external ad networks or brokers.

E. Data Storage, Cloud Infrastructure & Security (Section 39 NDPA)

Records are stored in secured cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.3). Our architecture enforces strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Anti-spam bot defenses (including server-side rate limits and honeypots) are deployed to protect form endpoints.

F. Data Retention Schedule

In accordance with data storage limitation rules:

  • Customer booking leads that do not convert into an active job dispatch are automatically purged after 90 days.
  • Completed job records are retained for statutory accounting and warranty dispute defense, after which they are securely deleted or pseudonymized.

G. Your Rights as a Data Subject (Part VI NDPA)

Under the NDPA 2023, you hold the following fundamental rights:

  • Right of Access & Rectification: Request a copy of your records or correct any inaccurate details.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request the complete deletion of your data from our systems.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: Restrict or object to further processing where applicable.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke processing consent at any time without retroactive penalty.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: If you believe your data has been mishandled, you have the statutory right to lodge an inquiry or complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email our Privacy Desk at: fix-plug.ng@gmail.com.

2. Terms of Service (Phase 1 Launch)

A. Service Status in Kano

FIX-PLUG operates an on-demand marketplace coordinating trade skills in Kano State. During our initial Phase 1 rollout, bookings enter our prioritized dispatch queue. Submitting a request initiates a booking dispatch review and does not constitute an unconditional guarantee of artisan availability.

B. Pricing, Estimates & Material Receipts

All preliminary price indications provided over phone or messaging are estimates. Final labor charges and required replacement materials are agreed directly on-site between customer and artisan after physical inspection before work commences. Customers pay verified store receipts for replacement materials.

C. Professional Standards & Respect

Both customers and artisans are expected to maintain professional and respectful conduct on every job. Vanishing artisans, unsafe work practices, or unfair price gouging result in immediate suspension from the FIX-PLUG network.

D. Corporate Registration Notice

FIX-PLUG is completing its corporate registration filings in Nigeria. Legal entity details and Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) numbers will appear in the platform footer upon official issuance.

E. Contact for Legal Inquiries

For all legal or regulatory inquiries, contact: fix-plug.ng@gmail.com.

OUR TRANSPARENT PROMISE

How Commission Works: Plain Words, Zero Tricks

We believe in honest partnerships. Here is our simple deal:

  • You pay ₦0 to register. Never give money to anyone claiming to represent FIX-PLUG for an application.
  • Fair transparent service fee: We agree on a simple, modest percentage on jobs dispatched through FIX-PLUG. The rest goes directly into your pocket.
  • Materials belong to the job: Cost of pipes, wires, or parts is kept 100% transparent with the customer receipt. We never take a cut of materials.

THE PROOF PAGE

Our Published Verification Standard

We don't use the word “verified” lightly. An artisan only enters the FIX-PLUG network after completing all 5 steps of our rigorous in-person standard in Kano.

Current status: We are currently conducting in-person vetting for our founding cohort of plumbers and electricians in Kano. We never dispatch an unvetted person to any customer.

The 5 Verification Gates

Every single check is verified with physical evidence, not just phone calls or online checkboxes.

1

Government ID & Biometric Verification

We physically inspect government-issued identification (National Identity Number - NIN slip, Voter's Card, or Driver's License). We match the photo, full legal name, and date of birth in person.

2

Kano Address & Contact Confirmation

We confirm the artisan's active residential address in Kano. We ensure they have stable local roots in communities like Nassarawa, Fagge, Tarauni, Dala, or Gwale.

3

Hands-on Practical Trade Skills Assessment

Certificates are good, but practical ability matters most. Each applicant performs live diagnostic tests evaluated by our Trade Vetting Lead—such as troubleshooting an electrical distribution board or correctly pressure-testing a plumbing valve.

4

Customer References & Work History

We interview at least two independent previous clients or master trainers where the artisan has completed work. We ask about punctuality, honesty, quality of finish, and dispute attitude.

5

Confirmed Local Guarantor

Every artisan must provide a recognized, reachable guarantor based in Kano (such as a business owner, community leader, civil servant, or landlord) who formally vouches for their character and reliability.

RESOLUTION POLICY

What Happens If Something Goes Wrong?

We don't disappear after introducing an artisan. If any job doesn't meet professional standards, here is our 3-step resolution process:

Step 1: Report Promptly

Send a message to our WhatsApp support or call our coordinator within 48 hours of work completion. Share photos or details of what is not working.

Step 2: Fair Technical Review

Our Vetting Lead reviews the work against the initial agreed job scope and talks to both the customer and the artisan to establish the facts impartially.

Step 3: Remediation

If the fault lies in poor workmanship, the artisan returns immediately to correct the issue at zero extra labor cost. If they fail to do so, FIX-PLUG assigns a senior pro.

Artisan accountability: An artisan who violates safety standards, leaves work abandoned, or engages in dishonesty is permanently removed from the FIX-PLUG roster.
